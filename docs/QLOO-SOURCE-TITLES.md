# Original Qloo film titles

New wide discoveries use protocol v12. Parsed provider film titles retain their
original characters, UUID and integer year. Repeated spaces, non-breaking spaces,
combining accents and NFKC-equivalent characters do not invalidate an otherwise
eligible film. The existing entity parser's outer whitespace trimming is unchanged;
the original HTTP samples and hashes remain intact. No model may invent or rename
a source film.

Both producer and independent verifier enforce a maximum of 300 code points on
the original title and its NFKC form, meaningful non-whitespace content, and reject
control/format/surrogate/private/unassigned characters and line/paragraph separators.
User queries retain their existing normalized text contract. Identity conflicts,
wrong types, years, exclusions, five-choice selection and source grounding remain
strict. Original decorative artwork is unchanged.

Read-only v11 verification uses frozen sources and semantics. Historical partial
captures remain partial; they are never resumed, resealed or converted to v12.
The earlier v3-v10 readers and sources remain byte-identical. A local synthetic
PASS does not prove new Qloo/Groq execution or cultural recommendation quality.
No new provider budget, publication or deployment is granted by this correction.
