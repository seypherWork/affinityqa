## Product and engineering scope

AffinityQA remains a diagnostic workspace for three controlled profile-routing
and cache faults, with independent recovery verification against recorded healthy
behavior. Its separate discovery interface takes one film or artist, optionally
one favorite film in artist mode, and returns five films. It has no artist-output
or song mode and makes no claim to repair arbitrary software.

The frozen local source passed **723 Windows tests**, with zero failures, errors
or skips, plus frontend production export, synthetic desktop/mobile browser
checks, source ZIP verification and wheel verification. Fourteen historical
captures passed read-only structural replay without changing their evidence.
The complete suite used isolated fixtures and blocked external provider access.
These are local engineering observations, not a new CI result.

## One actual v10 Eminem journey

An operator explicitly confirmed the Eminem music-artist identity, with no known
favorite, exclusions or extra direction. The case completed with **three Qloo
requests and one Groq Free selection** on `openai/gpt-oss-20b`. Two Qloo Insights
pages retained 100 candidate films; the model's entire accepted selection was
five closed index objects. Canonical UUIDs, titles and years were reconstructed
from the verified catalog, not echoed or invented by the model.

The delivered films were **8 Mile (2002)**, **Get Rich or Die Tryin' (2005)**,
**Notorious (2009)**, **Coach Carter (2005)** and **American Gangster (2007)**.
All five descriptions matched complete literal Spanish values in the associated
Qloo metadata. Descriptions did not travel to the model. The full request reserved
7,537 tokens including a 2,048-token completion allowance, below the unchanged
8,000-token limit. The observed completion used 102 tokens; there was no automatic
retry, model switch or schema fallback.

The five matching posters loaded. The native verification download was physically
saved and checked, and reloading/restoring recovered the same completed case
without new provider work. This is one operator-driven technical acceptance,
not a human preference study, a new native Massive Attack trial or a universal
model/seed reliability result.

## Preservation and the earlier failure

All **14 earlier cases** retained their original files, receipts, ownership,
session state and charge history. The immediately preceding v9 Eminem attempt
failed its exact echoed-title validation and remains **PARTIAL**. Its five unique
eligible indices did not authorize promotion or repair of that failed evidence;
the offending title wording was not retained and is not inferred here. The v10
case was a separately authorized fresh case.

These local cases are not being migrated to Render. Local and hosted histories
belong to separate stores and ownership sessions; release preparation does not
merge or replace them. Deployment must retain the hosted service's existing
cases independently of the retained local acceptance evidence.

Older generated prose is never reused by current cards. For example, the retained
Fighting (2009) result now displays the literal Qloo description linked to that
film, while the original historical receipt and verification download remain
unchanged. The source-based display is a derived top-level view, outside the
archived result. An archival notice makes this distinction visible.
